Strategy plan

How the strategy works

  1. Macro gate. New buys only while the market index is above its 200-day average (optionally also SMA50 above SMA200).
  2. Bull-run checks. A stock qualifies when all five pass: close above SMA50 and SMA200, SMA50 above SMA200, EMA12 above EMA26, RSI 14 inside the band, and the close within the set % of its 52-week high.
  3. Ranking. Qualifying stocks are ranked by their 6-month return; the strongest fill the free slots, at most sector cap per sector. New entries are considered every rebalance days.
  4. Exits. Sell when the close falls the trailing-stop % below its highest close since entry, or closes below SMA50.
  5. Sizing. Equal slots: each position gets equity ÷ slots. Risk-based: each position risks the set % of equity, with the stop at a multiple of the 20-day volatility, capped at the max position %. SET orders round down to 100-share lots.
  6. Price plan. Entry at the last close (buy zone down to EMA12), stop = the exit level, targets at 2R and 3R where R = entry − stop.

Statuses: BUY = would be bought at the next close · HOLD = in the model portfolio · EXIT = sell at the next close · WAIT = qualifies but blocked (no slot, sector cap or gate) · WATCH = 3–4 checks · AVOID = 2 or fewer. Education only, not investment advice.
